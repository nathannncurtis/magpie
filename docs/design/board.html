Brand

Ink and paper.
One shiny thing.

The mark is one brushstroke: pressed, dropped, swept under, lifted dry. It cradles one sheen bead, the collected thing. The sheen appears once per surface; everything else is ink and paper.

Concept · how the system behaves
Monochrome by defaultChrome, type, surfaces: ink on paper. In dark mode, paper fields on ink. Nothing decorative carries color.
The sheen is earnedThe gradient marks a collected thing — a quoted answer's source dot, an installed mod, the focused search field. It is never a background, never a button fill.
The voiceTerse and plain. Short sentences, no exclamation marks. It says what happened, not how it feels.
Color · five tokens, one gradient
Ink #101115

Chrome, marks, text, and the tile the mark sits on. In dark mode it becomes the ground.

Paper #F6F5F1

Surfaces, and the stroke itself when drawn on ink. Warm white — paper, not clinical white, not cream.

Sheen #0D9488 → #10B981 → #6EE7B7

Teal to mint, the tail's iridescence. Appears on exactly one element per surface.

Mist #E4E4DE

Hairlines, fills, quiet borders. Grey biased toward the sheen's cool side, never neutral-neutral.

The rule, stated once: the sheen marks the collected thing. The focused search field wears it as a 1.5px hairline; a found answer wears it as the source dot; an installed mod wears it in the mods list. It is never a background, never a fill, never a gradient hero. If a surface already carries a sheen, the next one waits. accent #0E9F73 · accent-ink #0B7A57 · dark #34C08E

Dark

Same screens after sunset. The mist turns to ink with the teal glowing through, glass goes smoky, and anything that was ink (toggles, icon squares, the primary pill) flips to paper. Sites that ship their own dark mode are left alone; Magpie never inverts a page.

Skim for me · photo tiers

Photos come only from the source pages' own preview images (og:image, else the article's largest image). No image search. The header adapts to what came back.

Edge states

The Installed tab, an empty list, and the three failures people will actually hit. Same pattern every time: one plain line, one way forward. The + on the tabs screen opens the launch search sheet on the mist (screen 01); it needs no screen of its own.

About the glass on this board

The board approximates Apple's Liquid Glass: each control gets a convex bezel that bends what sits behind it, a lit rim and a soft shadow. Chromium browsers show the bending; Safari and Firefox show a light blur. The app itself uses the real system material.

Motion

Seven moments, looping. Everything moves on springs, not timed curves; glass grows out of the thing you touched instead of appearing from nowhere; chrome follows the finger. The only bounce is a finished install. With Reduce Motion on, every one of these becomes a short crossfade.

Motion 01

Open search

Tap the address. The bar itself grows into the search sheet, quickly: it widens to the edges, rises, turns fully solid, and the keyboard comes up with it as one piece. Suggestions arrive a beat after the field, top to bottom. Swipe down or tap away and it shrinks back into the bar.

TriggerTap the address in the reading bar
MorphBar → sheet, one glass shape (GlassEffectContainer + matchedGeometry)
Springresponse 0.3, damping 0.88; about 0.5 s end to end
Fillglass turns fully solid as it grows; nothing shows through the open sheet
Fieldfades in 50 ms after the sheet starts
Suggestionsstagger 30 ms each
Keyboardsystem; rides the same spring

Cold start. The mist fades up, the mark draws itself once, then steps aside as the search sheet rises from the bottom, ready to type. On later launches Magpie opens straight to where you left off; this plays only when there's nowhere to return to.

App icon

Mark 6d as an iOS 26 icon: three layers (tile, stroke, bead) so Icon Composer can give each its own glass depth. The bead sits on top, so the light catches the find first. Previews here approximate Apple's rendering; the real highlights come from Icon Composer.
